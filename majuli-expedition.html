Post-ZFM Expedition: Majuli River Island

7 Days / 6 Nights · The Soulful River Escape

← All post-ZFM expeditions

Overview

The Soulful River Escape: Slow down on the world's largest inhabited river island. Cross the mighty Brahmaputra to explore ancient Neo-Vaishnavite monasteries (Satras), witness traditional mask-making heritage, and catch unforgettable sunsets over the water.

₹24,999

Pickup & Drop

  • Pickup: Dibrugarh Airport / Railway Station or Naharlagun Railway Station
  • Drop: Dibrugarh Airport / Railway Station / Hotel

Full Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Dibrugarh / Naharlagun → Ziro
    • Pickup from Dibrugarh Airport/Railway Station or Naharlagun Railway Station
    • Scenic drive through the beautiful valleys of Arunachal Pradesh
    • ILP verification (if applicable)
    • Check in to campsite/hotel
    • Evening free to explore Ziro town or relax at the campsite
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  2. Day 2: Ziro Music Festival + Local Exploration
    • Visit Shivalinga
    • Explore the serene Seeh Lake
    • Visit traditional Apatani villages
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy live music, local cuisine, handicrafts, and cultural performances
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  3. Day 3: Ziro Music Festival + Traditional Experience
    • Experience traditional Apatani fishing activity (seasonal)
    • Explore local cafés and artisan shops
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy evening headline performances
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  4. Day 4: Heritage Walk + Final Festival Day
    • Guided heritage village walk through Apatani settlements
    • Visit a local winery and experience indigenous fruit wines (optional tasting)
    • Spend the afternoon and evening enjoying the final day of the Ziro Music Festival
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  5. Day 5: Ziro → Majuli
    • Early breakfast
    • Arrive in Majuli
    • Check in to a traditional bamboo cottage/resort
    • Evening riverside walk and sunset
    • Overnight stay: Majuli homestay/resort
  6. Day 6: Explore Majuli
    • Visit Kamalabari Satra
    • Visit Auniati Satra
    • Explore Samaguri Satra and witness the famous mask-making tradition
    • Visit a Mishing tribal village
    • Explore pottery villages and local handicrafts
    • Sunset at the Brahmaputra riverbank
    • Overnight stay: Majuli homestay/resort
  7. Day 7: Majuli → Dibrugarh
    • Ferry back to Neemati Ghat
    • Scenic drive to Dibrugarh
    • Drop at Dibrugarh Airport/Railway Station/Hotel

Thoughtfully designed stays, warm hospitality, and unforgettable festival moments—experience Ziro the Wildnest way.
