ZFM + Nagaland Expedition

9 Days / 8 Nights · The Land of the Angami Kings

← All post-ZFM expeditions

Overview

The Land of the Angami Kings: Head south into the mystical hills of Kohima and the heritage village of Khonoma. Dive deep into the legendary history, terraced green valley vistas, and fiercely preserved clan traditions of the Naga warriors.

₹34,999

Pickup & Drop

  • Pickup: Guwahati Airport / Railway Station
  • Drop: Dimapur Airport / Railway Station (or Guwahati on request)

Full Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Guwahati → Ziro
    • Arrive at Guwahati and check in at rest house
    • Pickup from Guwahati rest house by evening
    • Arrive Ziro by morning and check in at campsite
    • Evening at leisure
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  2. Day 2: Ziro Music Festival + Local Exploration
    • Visit Shivalinga
    • Visit Seeh Lake
    • Explore traditional Apatani villages
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy live music, local cuisine, handicrafts, and cultural performances
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  3. Day 3: Ziro Music Festival + Traditional Experience
    • Traditional fishing activity in the Apatani paddy-cum-fish fields (seasonal)
    • Visit local cafés and artisan shops
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy the evening headline performances
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  4. Day 4: Heritage Walk + Final Festival Day
    • Guided heritage village walk through Apatani settlements
    • Visit a local winery and experience indigenous fruit wines (optional tasting)
    • Spend the afternoon and evening at the final day of the Ziro Music Festival
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  5. Day 5: Ziro → Kohima
    • Early breakfast
    • Scenic drive to Nagaland via Assam
    • Arrive in Kohima
    • Visit the Kohima War Cemetery
    • Evening café hopping or local market exploration
    • Overnight stay: Kohima hotel
  6. Day 6: Kisama Heritage Village & Jakhama Village
    • Breakfast
    • Visit Kisama Heritage Village
    • Explore the traditional Naga Morungs
    • Learn about the rich heritage of Nagaland's tribes
    • Continue to Jakhama Village
    • Guided village walk
    • Interact with local families
    • Experience Naga cuisine and rural lifestyle
    • Overnight stay: Kigwema homestay
  7. Day 7: Khonoma Green Village
    • Excursion to Khonoma, India's first Green Village
    • Guided heritage walk
    • Explore Angami tribal houses
    • Visit terraced paddy fields
    • Nature walk through forest trails
    • Learn about Khonoma's conservation initiatives
    • Overnight stay: Khonoma homestay
  8. Day 8: Dzuleke Nature Retreat
    • Drive to the picturesque Dzuleke Village
    • Explore the riverside landscapes
    • Nature walk through forests and streams
    • Birdwatching and photography
    • Experience authentic village life
    • Return to Kohima in the evening
    • Overnight stay: Dzuleke homestay
  9. Day 9: Kohima → Dimapur
    • Leisure time for souvenir shopping (subject to departure schedule)
    • Drive to Dimapur
    • Drop at Dimapur Airport/Railway Station

Thoughtfully designed stays, warm hospitality, and unforgettable festival moments—experience Ziro the Wildnest way.
