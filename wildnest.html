The Wildnest Experience

Premium festival camping — comfort, community, and culture in Ziro Valley.

Here is exactly what sets the Wildnest experience apart:

🏕️

The Ultimate Campsite Infrastructure

  • Weatherproof Comfort: Premium, genuinely waterproof tents designed to keep you warm and dry through Ziro's moody weather. Every setup is loaded with cozy pillows, clean blankets, and insulation tailored to your stay — featuring thick, foldable mattresses in our Premium/Alpine tents, and heavy-duty, black-foam sleeping mats in our classic Dome tents to lock out the ground chill.
  • Modern Amenities: Impeccably clean, segregated concrete restrooms offering both Western and Indian facilities, backed by 24/7 clean running water.
  • Zero Blackouts: Full 24/7 power backup via dual DG generators, ensuring your devices stay charged and the camp stays brilliantly lit.
  • Prime Location: Situated just a short, scenic easy stroll from the main festival gates along a well-connected road.
🍳

Gourmet Dining, Fun Zone & Late-Night Vibes

  • Complimentary Meals: Wake up to a hearty breakfast and wind down with a warm homely dinner (with excellent Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian options included in your stay).
  • In-House Café & Bar: Skip the festival food lines! Our camp features a fully operational café and bar managed by professional chefs serving custom menus, with lunch available via pre-orders.
  • The Wildnest Fun Zone & Creative Hub: Kill time before the festival gates open or enjoy a lazy afternoon in our dedicated community hangout zone — classic board games, cards, quirky multiplayer party games, and a creative corner for festival posters, painting, or swapping DIY thread bands with fellow campers.
  • The After-Hours Magic: When the main stages close, our central dining area transforms into an exclusive hub for nightly bonfires, acoustic jam sessions under the stars, and late-night drinks with a tight-knit community of music lovers.
🗺️

Curated Local Adventures Optional Add-ons

Want to explore Ziro beyond the music? We host curated local excursions so you can immerse yourself in the valley's rich heritage:

  • Apatani Tribal Walks: Guided cultural walks through local villages to learn about indigenous traditions and sustainable living.
  • Farm-to-Table Experiences: Try traditional bare-hand fishing and learn about local cultivation culture (you can even try your hand at planting paddy!).
  • Hidden Gems: Guided day trips to the serene Seeh Lake, the world's highest naturally formed Shiva Lingam, and the famous local winery featured on Shark Tank India.
🛡️

24/7 Hospitality & Safety

Our on-ground crew consists of highly seasoned travel professionals. Your comfort and safety are our absolute number one priority, with dedicated team members available around the clock to assist you.
