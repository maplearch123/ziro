ZFM + Anini Expedition

10 Days / 9 Nights · The Untamed Frontier

← All post-ZFM expeditions

Overview

The Untamed Frontier: For the true explorer. Journey into the raw, misty, and untouched valleys of Dibang—a land of roaring glacial rivers, absolute isolation, and the rawest edge of Northeast India.

₹36,999

Pickup & Drop

  • Pickup & drop: Dibrugarh Airport / Railway Station

Full Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Dibrugarh → Ziro
    • Pickup from Dibrugarh Airport/Railway Station
    • Cross the iconic Bogibeel Bridge
    • Drive through Dhemaji and North Lakhimpur
    • Scenic drive through forests, villages, and paddy fields
    • Arrive in Ziro and check in to the campsite/hotel
    • Evening at leisure
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  2. Day 2: Ziro Music Festival + Local Exploration
    • Visit Shivalinga
    • Visit the serene Seeh Lake
    • Explore nearby Apatani villages
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy live performances, local cuisine, art installations, and cultural activities
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  3. Day 3: Ziro Music Festival + Fishing Experience
    • Breakfast
    • Traditional fishing activity in the Apatani paddy-cum-fish fields (seasonal and subject to availability)
    • Leisure time to explore cafés or local markets
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Experience the evening headline performances
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  4. Day 4: Heritage & Culture Experience
    • Breakfast
    • Guided heritage village walk through traditional Apatani settlements
    • Visit a local winery to learn about indigenous fruit wines and enjoy a tasting session (optional)
    • Spend the afternoon at the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy the final festival performances
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  5. Day 5: Ziro → Roing
    • Early breakfast
    • Scenic drive through Arunachal Pradesh
    • River valleys, forests, and mountain landscapes
    • Photography stops en route
    • Arrive in Roing
    • Evening at leisure
    • Overnight stay: Roing hotel
  6. Day 6: Roing → Anini
    • Breakfast
    • Drive through the spectacular Dibang Valley
    • Pass waterfalls, dense forests, and the Dibang River
    • Scenic viewpoints and photography stops
    • Arrive in Anini
    • Overnight stay: Anini homestay/resort
  7. Day 7: Explore Mathun Valley
    • Breakfast
    • Full-day excursion to the breathtaking Mathun Valley
    • Visit picturesque riverside viewpoints
    • Explore remote Idu Mishmi settlements
    • Nature walks and photography
    • Return to Anini by evening
    • Overnight stay: Anini homestay/resort
  8. Day 8: Explore Dri Valley
    • Breakfast
    • Visit the beautiful Dri Valley
    • Explore Chigu Camp
    • Visit Bruni
    • Explore nearby waterfalls and natural streams
    • Picnic and photography amidst pristine landscapes
    • Return to Anini
    • Overnight stay: Anini homestay/resort
  9. Day 9: Anini → Dibrugarh
    • Early breakfast
    • Drive back via Roing
    • Cross the Bhupen Hazarika (Dhola–Sadiya) Bridge
    • Arrive in Dibrugarh by evening
    • Check in to the hotel
    • Overnight stay: Dibrugarh
  10. Day 10: Departure
    • Breakfast
    • Check out from the hotel
    • Drop at Dibrugarh Airport/Railway Station

Thoughtfully designed stays, warm hospitality, and unforgettable festival moments—experience Ziro the Wildnest way.
