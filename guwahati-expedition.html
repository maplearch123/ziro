Route A · Wildnest Adventures 2026

The Guwahati Premium Expedition

5 Nights / 6 Days · Full overland loop from Guwahati

← All routes

Timeline

Departs from Guwahati on the evening of September 23, 2026 (overnight transit) · Returns to Guwahati on September 28, 2026.

The Vibe

Arrive in Guwahati by 12:00 PM on Day 1. We have arranged a comfortable base lounge for the entire crew to freshen up, drop off heavy luggage, and break the ice with fellow travellers before we hit the road for our overnight journey.

The Full Loop Itinerary

  1. Day 1 (Sept 23): Reach Guwahati, unwind at our dedicated central lounge, and kick off the overnight road trip into the mountains in a comfortable shared vehicle.
  2. Day 2 (Sept 24): Wake up to crisp mountain air, cross the border into the easternmost state of India, and check into our campsite. Catch Day 1 of the Festival and wrap up with our complimentary warm camp dinner around the hearth.
  3. Day 3 (Sept 25): Fuel up with our wholesome complimentary breakfast, head out for the Sidheshwar Mahadev & Seeh Lake to witness the world's highest naturally formed Shiva Lingam and the pine-ringed serene lake, hit the festival stages all afternoon, and return to camp for our complimentary dinner and signature after-hours acoustic jamming session under a star-loaded sky.
  4. Day 4 (Sept 26): Start your morning with a delicious complimentary camp breakfast before diving into the Traditional Paddy Field Fishing experience in the morning and learn the ancient Apatani art of catching fish with your bare hands. The afternoon belongs to the festival music, followed by an intimate night bonfire back at base camp alongside another chef-curated complimentary dinner.
  5. Day 5 (Sept 27): Enjoy your complimentary morning breakfast before embarking on the Ziro Heritage & Winery Trail in the morning to explore UNESCO heritage villages, dive deep into the Apatani Tribal Experience. The trail culminates at the famous, family-run Naara Aaba Winery (of Shark Tank India fame), where you'll tour the facilities and savor an exclusive tasting of organically sourced, award-winning kiwi and wildberry wines. Spend the evening witnessing the grand closing ceremony of ZMF 2026, followed by a final community dinner.
  6. Day 6 (Sept 28): Pack up your memories and board your pre-arranged shuttle back to Guwahati for your drop-off.

Note

Want an upgrade? Give our team a shout during checkout, and we can hook you up with a private vehicle and a dedicated chauffeur for an extra fee.
