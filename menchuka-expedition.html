Post-ZFM Expedition · Wildnest Adventures 2026

ZFM + Menchuka Expedition

9 Days / 8 Nights · The Forbidden Shangri-La

← All post-ZFM expeditions

Overview

The Forbidden Shangri-La: Travel to a surreal, snow-ringed valley near the Tibetan border. Walk through low-hanging clouds, step inside a 400-year-old Buddhist monastery, cross wooden suspension bridges, and experience the warm hospitality of the Memba tribe.

₹36,999

Pickup & Drop

  • Pickup: Dibrugarh Airport / Railway Station (Naharlagun pickup available on request)
  • Drop: Dibrugarh Airport / Railway Station

Full Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Dibrugarh → Ziro
    • Pickup from Dibrugarh Airport/Railway Station
    • Cross the iconic Bogibeel Bridge
    • Scenic drive through Dhemaji and North Lakhimpur
    • Check in to campsite
    • Evening at leisure
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  2. Day 2: Ziro Music Festival + Local Exploration
    • Visit Shivalinga
    • Visit Seeh Lake
    • Explore traditional Apatani villages
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy live music, local cuisine, handicrafts, and cultural performances
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  3. Day 3: Ziro Music Festival + Traditional Experience
    • Traditional Apatani fishing activity (seasonal)
    • Explore local cafés and artisan markets
    • Attend the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy evening performances by renowned artists
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  4. Day 4: Heritage Walk + Final Festival Day
    • Guided heritage village walk through Apatani settlements
    • Visit a local winery (optional tasting)
    • Spend the afternoon and evening at the final day of the Ziro Music Festival
    • Overnight stay: camping at Ziro
  5. Day 5: Ziro → Aalo
    • Early breakfast
    • Scenic drive through dense forests and mountain roads
    • Stop at rivers, hanging bridges, and panoramic viewpoints
    • Explore Aalo town in the evening
    • Enjoy the Siyom River views
    • Overnight stay: Aalo hotel
  6. Day 6: Aalo → Mechuka
    • Drive through one of Arunachal Pradesh's most scenic mountain routes
    • Pass waterfalls, pine forests, and river valleys
    • Stop at scenic viewpoints for photography
    • Arrive in Mechuka and check in
    • Evening walk along the Siyom River
    • Overnight stay: Mechuka homestay
  7. Day 7: Explore Mechuka
    • Visit the historic Samten Yongcha Monastery (400-year-old Buddhist monastery)
    • Explore Dorjeeling Village
    • Visit the Mechuka Hanging Bridge
    • Relax by the Siyom River
    • Explore local cafés and the town market
    • Enjoy sunset views over the valley
    • Overnight stay: Mechuka homestay
  8. Day 8: Mechuka Adventure & Village Experience
    • Visit the Indo–China Border View Point (subject to permits and road conditions)
    • Explore Hanuman Point
    • Visit traditional Memba tribal villages
    • Optional cycling, riverside walk, or nature photography
    • Experience local cuisine and culture
    • Overnight stay: Mechuka homestay
  9. Day 9: Mechuka → Dibrugarh
    • Return drive to Dibrugarh
    • Photography stops along the Siyom Valley
    • Evening free to relax or explore the local market
    • Overnight stay: Dibrugarh hotel

Thoughtfully designed stays, warm hospitality, and unforgettable festival moments—experience Ziro the Wildnest way.
