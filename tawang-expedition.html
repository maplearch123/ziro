ZFM + Tawang Expedition

10 Days / 9 Nights · The Cultural High Road

← All post-ZFM expeditions

Overview

The Cultural High Road: Keep the adventure alive! Journey deep into the Monpa heartland to conquer the majestic, snow-dusted Sela Pass, witness frozen alpine lakes, and explore India’s largest ancient monastery.

₹36,999

Full Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Guwahati → Ziro (Approx. 450 km | 9–10 hrs)
    • Pickup from Guwahati Airport/Railway Station
    • Scenic drive through North Lakhimpur into Arunachal Pradesh
    • Check in to campsite/homestay
    • Evening at leisure
    • Dinner & overnight stay in Ziro
  2. Day 2: Ziro Music Festival – Day 1
    • Visit Apatani villages (Hong/Hari) if time permits
    • Full-day access to the Ziro Music Festival
    • Enjoy live performances, food stalls and cultural experiences
    • Dinner
    • Overnight camping at Ziro
  3. Day 3: Ziro Music Festival – Day 2
    • Breakfast
    • Siddheshwar Nath temple visit and Seeh Lake
    • Attend festival performances
    • Bonfire (optional)
    • Dinner
    • Overnight camping at Ziro
  4. Day 4: Ziro Music Festival – Day 3
    • Fishing activity in the paddy fields of Ziro Valley
    • Festival access throughout the day
    • Evening headline performances
    • Dinner
    • Overnight camping at Ziro
  5. Day 5: Ziro Music Festival – Day 4
    • Breakfast
    • Village walk through heritage village of Ziro and Winery visit
    • Festival access throughout the day
    • Evening headline performances
    • Dinner
    • Overnight camping at Ziro
  6. Day 6: Ziro → Dirang (Approx. 340 km | 9–10 hrs)
    • Drive via Itanagar, Bhalukpong and Bomdila
    • En route mountain viewpoints
    • Check in at Dirang
    • Evening walk around Dirang town
    • Dinner & overnight stay
  7. Day 7: Dirang → Tawang
    • Cross Sela Pass
    • Visit Sela Lake
    • Visit Jaswant Garh War Memorial
    • Reach Tawang
    • Evening visit to local market
    • Overnight in Tawang
  8. Day 8: Tawang Local Sightseeing
    • Visit Tawang Monastery
    • Bum La Pass
    • Sangetsar Lake (Madhuri Lake)
    • P.T. Tso Lake
    • Overnight stay in Tawang
  9. Day 9: Tawang → Bomdila
    • Breakfast
    • Return via Sela Pass
    • Visit Nuranang Falls
    • Reach Bomdila
    • Visit Bomdila Monastery (subject to time)
    • Overnight stay
  10. Day 10: Bomdila → Guwahati (Approx. 340 km | 8–9 hrs)
    • Scenic drive back to Guwahati
    • Drop at Airport/Railway Station
    • Tour ends with unforgettable memories
